# Partial moments in Lean

This library checks claims about partial moments in the NNS book and package documentation,
including formulas for tail probabilities, covariance and stochastic dominance. Each table entry
links to the passage and the Lean theorem with its assumptions.

## Build

With [elan](https://lean-lang.org/install/) installed, run the following from the NNS repository.

```sh
cd lean
./get-cache.sh
lake build --wfail
```

The toolchain and Mathlib versions are pinned. To update the index after editing a proof or
`claims.tsv`, run `python3 generate_readme.py` from this directory.

## Claim index

The index covers 398 claims. Lean proves 367 as stated
and 14 with conditions. It gives counterexamples for
16. C303 has a partial proof.

### Counterexamples

The links show each passage as it read before the corrections in the book.

- C090. Symmetry is sufficient for the halved Chebyshev bound. The asymmetric sample (−1, −1, 2) also satisfies it at every threshold below its mean. [Ch. 4, 278](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-04-numerical-integration-via-partial-moments.Rmd#L278)
- C096. The bound includes the endpoint g = t. For a point mass at t the event has probability one and the displayed quotient divides zero by zero. [Ch. 4, 286-292](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-04-numerical-integration-via-partial-moments.Rmd#L286-L292)
- C099. Cantelli gives a tighter bound at −50 for a sample with one loss of 99 and 99 gains of one. [Ch. 4, 317](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-04-numerical-integration-via-partial-moments.Rmd#L317)
- C122. The sample (−5, −5, 1, 3, 6) extends farther to the right of its mean and has negative skewness. [Ch. 5, 190-197](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-05-classical-moments-as-directional-aggregates.Rmd#L190-L197)
- C147. A distribution can have finite absolute mean and infinite third directional moments. Integrability alone does not justify the third-moment identity. [Ch. 6, 216](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-06-measure-theoretic-interpretation.Rmd#L216)
- C252. Keeping both marginals fixed and reversing the pairing can change the normalized joint lower weight from one half to zero. [Ch. 13, 401](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-13-conditional-probability-and-bayes-theorem.Rmd#L401)
- C274. The degree-one 5% threshold lies between the 25th and 26th population percentiles for an exponential law. In every sample of two distinct observations, half the sample lies below that threshold. [Ch. 15, 224](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-15-distribution-comparison.Rmd#L224)
- C309. A constant group and a variable group with the same mean agree centrally in exact arithmetic. Rounding the pooled mean in the example changes the means-only certainty from 4/9 to zero. [Ch. 15, 578](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-15-distribution-comparison.Rmd#L578)
- C333. Two identical samples pass the weak CDF inequality at every threshold. First-order dominance also requires strict inequality somewhere. [Ch. 15, 801-805](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-15-distribution-comparison.Rmd#L801-L805)
- C338. Failure of the forward CDF test can mean dominance in the reverse direction. Point masses at zero and one give this case. [Ch. 15, 829-835](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-15-distribution-comparison.Rmd#L829-L835)
- C344. A cyclic second-order dominance matrix can put strictly ordered pairs in the first fully peeled cluster. The antichain claim requires acyclicity. [Ch. 15, 866](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-15-distribution-comparison.Rmd#L866)
- C365. The asymmetric sample (−1, −1, 2) satisfies the halved Chebyshev bound at every lower threshold, refuting the necessity of symmetry. [Ch. 16, 160](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-16-directional-tail-thresholds-probability-bounds-and-estimation-error.Rmd#L160)
- C368. At g = t a point mass at t has lower-tail probability one. The proposed upper bound has a zero denominator. [Ch. 16, 176-179](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-16-directional-tail-thresholds-probability-bounds-and-estimation-error.Rmd#L176-L179)
- C427. At degree zero the lower ratio counts observations equal to the target. A count strictly below the target misses ties. [Ch. 28, 60](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/book/chapter-28-appendix-notation-and-function-reference.Rmd#L60)
- C490. Two samples can give the same four outputs of NNS.moments and different upper third and fourth partial moments. Those outputs do not determine the separate tail diagnostics. [NNSvignette_01_Overview.Rmd, 110-118](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/vignettes/NNSvignette_01_Overview.Rmd#L110-L118)
- C555. For two distinct observations a < b, the degree-one 5% threshold is (19a + b)/20. Exactly one of the two observations lies below it. [LPM.VaR.Rd, 12](https://github.com/OVVO-Financial/NNS/blob/98bc42ea/man/LPM.VaR.Rd#L12)
